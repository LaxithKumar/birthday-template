AKSHITHA BIRTHDAY SURPRISE — V3

1. Open index.html in a browser.
2. Click the secret image to unlock.
3. Click any editable text and type your own memories/personal words.
4. Click or drag photos onto the photo slots.
5. Click “Save my finished website”.
6. A file named akshitha-birthday-surprise.html will download with your text and photos embedded.
7. Rename it to index.html and upload it to your GitHub Pages repository.

Important: the upload slots work in the browser. The Save button is what makes the chosen photos and edits part of the HTML you publish.
